MINDBRIDGE SQUARE ICONS (1:1)

Every file is square. Each folder has one SVG (scales to any size) and PNGs at
1024, 512, 256, 192, 180, 128, 64, 48, 32 and 16 px.

  symbol/        Symbol only, transparent background. For white or light backgrounds.
  symbol-dark/   Symbol only, transparent background. For black or dark backgrounds.
  icon-white/    Symbol on a white square.
  icon-black/    Symbol on a black square.
  icon-rounded/  Symbol on a black rounded square (app-icon style).
  avatar-white/  Symbol on a white circle, padded so a circular crop never cuts it.
  avatar-black/  Symbol on a black circle. Use these two for social media profile pictures.
  favicon.ico    16, 32 and 48 px in one file, for websites.

Colors: gold #C8B48A, grey #A6A6A6 (#8A8A8A on dark), black #000000, white #FFFFFF.
