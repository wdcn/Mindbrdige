MINDBRIDGE LOGO KIT

Folders
  svg/      Vector files. Use these for print, signage and anything that needs to scale.
            Text is converted to outlines, so no font needs to be installed.
  png/      Transparent images for documents, slides and social posts.
  favicon/  Website icons: favicon.ico, favicon.svg, favicon-16/32/48.png,
            apple-touch-icon.png (180), icon-192.png and icon-512.png.

Versions
  mindbridge-logo-horizontal   Symbol + wordmark side by side. Main logo.
  mindbridge-logo-stacked      Symbol above wordmark. For square spaces.
  mindbridge-wordmark          Wordmark only.
  mindbridge-mark              Symbol only (two speech bubbles).
  mindbridge-app-icon          Symbol on a black rounded square.

  Files ending in "-dark" are for black or dark backgrounds (white text).
  Files without it are for white or light backgrounds (black text).

Colors
  Black        #000000   "Mind", where the bubbles overlap
  Gold         #C8B48A   left bubble, triangle dot on the i
  Grey         #A6A6A6   right bubble (on light backgrounds)
  Grey, dark   #8A8A8A   right bubble (on dark backgrounds)
  Text grey    #6F6F6F   "Bridge" (on light backgrounds)
  Light grey   #BDBDBD   "Bridge" (on dark backgrounds)

Typeface
  Instrument Serif (SIL Open Font License). "Mind" in Regular, "Bridge" in Italic.

Use
  Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the triangle dot x 2.
  Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects.
  Smallest size: horizontal logo 120 px wide on screen; below that use the symbol or app icon.
